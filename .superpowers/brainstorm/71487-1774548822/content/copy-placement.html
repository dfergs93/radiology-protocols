Where should the "Copy link" affordance live?

The URL auto-updates as you pick protocols. These options show how/where users are made aware they can share it.

A

Button in the selector toolbar

A 🔗 Copy Link button sits alongside Compare / Clear / Add Protocol. Always visible once 2+ protocols are selected. Clicking it copies the current URL to clipboard and shows a brief "Copied!" confirmation.

+ Add Protocol Compare Clear 🔗 Copy Link
B

Floating button in results area

After comparison results appear, a small 🔗 Share button floats in the top-right corner of the results section. Only visible when results are shown.

C

No button — toast notification only

When comparison runs, a brief toast appears: "Link updated — copy from address bar". No persistent button. Clean UI, but relies on users knowing to copy the address bar.
